Context & Crisis

The landscape is littered with memory, yet our registries remain sparse. Over 1,300 structural anomalies and undocumented monuments have been identified through satellite telemetry and archival fragmentation, but a mere 174 currently receive formal protection and study.

The gap between what exists and what is recorded is not just a bureaucratic oversight; it is an active erasure of regional history. Without a rigorous framework for synthesis, these sites will succumb to development, weather, and time.

The Methodology

THRESHOLD operates as a semantic bridge. It algorithmically reads disparate archival records, cross-references mention vectors, and projects them onto the physical landscape.

We do not merely map; we substantiate. By correlating historical text with spatial coordinates, the system generates high-probability candidate sites, transforming static archives into dynamic fieldwork itineraries.
