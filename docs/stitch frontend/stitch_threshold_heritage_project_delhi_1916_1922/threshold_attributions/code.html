THRESHOLD relies exclusively on verifiable, open-source archival material and contemporary documentary photography. No imagery of heritage points is computationally generated. This registry details the provenance, authorship, and licensing of all visual and typographic assets integrated into the landscape record.

The Record Taxonomy // 02
Archival Volume
The core corpus of historical texts, survey reports, and field notes utilized to construct the narrative framework of THRESHOLD. All texts are verified against primary sources held in recognized national archives.
Baseline Pull
The foundational topographical dataset derived from historical OS maps, used to anchor all heritage points within a verifiable geospatial coordinate system, ensuring historical accuracy of location data.
Zone Footprints
Delineated boundaries representing the historical extent of structural foundations or environmental features, constructed by cross-referencing aerial photography with archaeological survey reports.
Voices & Types
Typographic selections are intentional. EB Garamond evokes the humanist tradition of early 20th-century printing, while IBM Plex Mono handles structural and metadata elements, nodding to modern archival classification systems.

What is not here.

THRESHOLD operates strictly on a principle of evidential sourcing. Therefore, you will not find:

  • ■ Generative AI reconstitutions of lost structures.
  • ■ Speculative environmental modeling without peer-reviewed geological backing.
  • ■ Unattributed vernacular photography.

The integrity of the record supersedes aesthetic convenience.
